# Florida distress filings by county (monthly)

Monthly counts of lis pendens (pre-foreclosure) filings delivered by Lispend in 10 Florida counties, July to October 2026, plus monthly probate filing totals for 5 counties. Measured 2026-11-19 from county clerk records, counting customer-visible filings by recording or filing date.

Files: `lis_pendens_by_county.csv` (county, month, filings) and `probate_totals.csv` (month, filings, counties).

Counts reflect the counties Lispend covers and grow as coverage grows, so read month-to-month change within a county, not statewide totals. For a statewide measure, ATTOM’s 2026 mid-year report counts 27,494 Florida properties with foreclosure filings, up 33% from a year earlier.

Source and methods: https://www.lispend.com/reports/florida-distress-report

Lispend is a data service for real estate investors and wholesalers that collects distressed-property filings in Florida and California from county records and matches each one to the owner and mailing address.
